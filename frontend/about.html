AI-Powered Food Waste Donation & Redistribution Platform

An academic project by Team 56 at Bennett University, India (Manu Bhardwaj, S24BCAU0034; Amul Sharma, S24BCAU0013). FoodRescue AI is the working prototype: it connects food donors with NGOs and food banks and coordinates volunteer deliveries.

How recommendations are calculated

The decision engine is an explainable, AI-assisted weighted scoring model. It is not a trained machine-learning model: every score is a transparent combination of five named factors with fixed weights, so each recommendation can be explained and audited.

1. Hard feasibility checks

An NGO is removed from consideration if any of these is true:

  • it does not accept the donation's food category;
  • its available capacity is zero or smaller than the donation;
  • it has no unmet demand;
  • it is outside the service radius (25 km by default) or has invalid coordinates;
  • it already declined this donation.

The donation itself must be open, not expired, and have a valid pickup window that has not closed. Optionally, an available volunteer must be able to reach it.

2. Five scoring components

Freshness
F = 1 - min(T, 24) / 24, T = hours of usable life left
Proximity
G = exp(-D / 5), D = Haversine distance in km
Capacity fit
C = min(q, c) / max(q, c)
Demand fit
Q = min(q, d) / max(q, d)
Priority
P in [0, 1], set per NGO

Final score S = wF F + wG G + wC C + wQ Q + wP P, ranked highest first.

Proximity dominates, so nearby NGOs are strongly preferred; the other factors break ties and promote urgent food, good size fit and high-priority recipients.

Donation lifecycle

  1. Posteddonor lists the food
  2. Matchedranked NGOs found
  3. AcceptedNGO reserves capacity
  4. Picked upvolunteer confirms
  5. Deliveredvolunteer confirms

Open or accepted donations can be cancelled by the donor, and expire automatically when their usable life runs out. Cancelling or expiry releases any capacity and demand the NGO had reserved. Every state change is written to an audit log.

What is real, simulated, or future work

Working in this application

  • Registration, login and role-based access for donors, NGOs, volunteers and admins
  • The weighted matching engine with explanations
  • Atomic acceptance that prevents double allocation
  • Volunteer tasks with timestamped pickup and delivery
  • In-app notifications, reports and an audit log

Simulated or not included

  • Benchmark results come from a synthetic dataset (seed 7), not a real deployment
  • Distances are straight-line; there is no road-network routing
  • No food-safety certification: people must inspect the food
  • No SMS or email delivery; notifications are in-app only
  • Demo organisations and accounts are fictional
