The problem

Cooked meals and fresh produce lose value by the hour. Most surplus is thrown away not because nobody needs it, but because the right recipient isn't found in time.

Time runs out

Prepared meals may be usable for only a few hours. A slow phone tree means food expires before anyone collects it.

Mismatched needs

A food bank may lack cold storage for dairy, or already be full. Sending food there just moves the waste.

No one to carry it

Even a good match fails without a volunteer who can reach the pickup point in time with enough vehicle space.

How it works

Every recommendation shows its score and the reason behind it. Final decisions stay with people.

  1. Post

    A donor lists the food, quantity, remaining usable life, pickup point and window.

  2. Match

    Infeasible NGOs are filtered out, then the rest are ranked by a weighted score with a plain-language explanation.

  3. Accept

    The NGO accepts or declines. Capacity and demand are reserved atomically, so food is never double-allocated.

  4. Deliver

    A nearby available volunteer accepts the task and confirms pickup and delivery, with timestamps.
